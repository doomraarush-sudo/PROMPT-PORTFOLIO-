AI Prompt Portfolio
Finance, HR, Marketing & Sales — AI/ML Business Prompt Collection

This repository contains four AI Prompt Portfolios covering how Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning can be applied across:

💰 Finance
👥 Human Resources
📣 Marketing
📈 Sales

Each portfolio contains 25 prompts, giving a total of 100 prompts. The prompts progress from broad/basic questions to advanced business problem-solving and strategic AI applications. The source documents use this five-tier progression across the portfolios.

🎯 Project Objective

The objective is to demonstrate how prompt engineering can progressively narrow a broad AI topic into a specific, structured and business-focused AI/ML problem.

The overall progression is:

Broad Exploration → Industry/Domain Focus → Specific Business Problem → Solution Design & Critical Evaluation → Strategic & Expert-Level Synthesis

The prompts progressively introduce techniques such as:

Role assignment
Structured outputs
Real-world examples
Source requests
Risk and ethics evaluation
Self-verification
Implementation roadmaps
Executive-level business cases

🧩 Prompt Structure
Tier 1 — Broad Exploration

Introduces the basics of AI and its applications within each business domain.

Tier 2 — Industry / Domain Focus

Narrows the topic and introduces roles, comparisons and real-world examples.

Tier 3 — Specific Business Problem

Focuses on a concrete AI/ML business problem and asks for structured, justified analysis.

Tier 4 — Solution Design & Critical Evaluation

Moves into solution architecture, risks, ethics, responsible AI, sourcing and critical evaluation.

Tier 5 — Strategic & Expert-Level Synthesis

Uses executive-level scenarios involving:

Executive summaries
Self-verification
Stress testing
12-month implementation roadmaps
Responsible AI frameworks
Business cases

The Finance portfolio, for example, moves from broad finance questions to loan-default architecture, responsible AI, implementation planning and a CFO-level business case.

🧠 What This Project Demonstrates

The project demonstrates how a prompt can evolve from a simple question into a sophisticated business-AI request:

Context → Role → Business Problem → Data → AI/ML Approach → Output Format → Risks → Verification → Business Action

The portfolios also incorporate responsible AI considerations, including privacy, bias, transparency, human oversight and regulatory/ethical concerns.
